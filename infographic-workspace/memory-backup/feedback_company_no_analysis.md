Company (Cu Circuits) infographic posts must NEVER contain:
- Predictions or forecasts from the company's perspective
- Personal analysis or opinion (e.g. "this is a margin strategy, not supply shortage")
- Statements that could be interpreted as the company's official position on market trends
- Any language that could incriminate or create liability for the company

**Why:** A company posting analysis about competitors' pricing strategies or market predictions creates legal/business risk. "Cu Circuits says TI is doing margin enhancement" is very different from a personal account sharing the same analysis.

**How to apply:**
- Company posts should share FACTS ONLY: what happened, who announced what, specific numbers
- Move all analysis, insight, opinion, and predictions to the CAPTION (LinkedIn post text) only if phrased as industry reporting, not company opinion
- Better yet, keep company posts purely informational: data, dates, facts
- Personal account posts CAN freely include analysis, predictions, contrarian takes, and opinions
- When writing company infographics, remove any "insight" section that contains analysis
- Captions for company posts should ask questions to the audience rather than state opinions
